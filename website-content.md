### 🚨 CONTENT GENERATION RULES (CRITICAL)
Before generating any website content in the following steps, you MUST adhere to these strict formatting rules for EVERY section:

1. **Dual-Heading Structure:** Every single section MUST have a category heading (H2) followed immediately by a conversational subheading (H3).
2. **Category Heading (H2):** The H2 must be exactly 2 to 4 words long. It should describe the topic (e.g., "Cost Breakdown", "Local Expertise", "Core Services"). Do NOT use meta-labels like "Hero Section", "Trust Section", or "About Us Section".
3. **Conversational Subheading (H3):** The H3 below the category heading should be a natural, conversational sentence or question (e.g., "What is the cost of PVC in Sydney?", "Why choose our experienced team?"). 
4. **Short Paragraphs:** Do NOT write large paragraphs. Keep paragraphs extremely short (1 to 2 sentences maximum) and use bullet points frequently to ensure scannability.
5. **Total Word Count:** The entire generated page content must be strictly between 900 to 1200 words.

**Example of correct section formatting:**
## Cost Breakdown
### What is the cost of PVC in Sydney?
[Short 1-2 sentence paragraph or bullet points go here...]

### STEP 3 — Generate Comprehensive Outline Structure
Create a detailed outline following these structural points. **(You must apply the 🚨 CONTENT GENERATION RULES from above to every single section)**:

#### Option A: Standard Service & Landing Pages
1. **Hero**: Outcome-focused dual headings. Include exactly three high-impact reassurance bullet points and clear call-to-actions.
2. **AI Overview / About**: Dual headings optimized for seamless LLM extraction.
3. **Problem / Symptom / Need**: Dual headings detailing signs/problems leading to the service. 
4. **Service / Product Types**: Dual headings. Focused list of 4-5 core offerings max. Include internal links to deeper pages and a "View All Services" CTA.
5. **Mid-Page CTA Banner**: Dual headings. Positioned immediately after the services section. Employs a banner heading, sub-text, and an action-oriented CTA button.
6. **Process / How It Works**: Dual headings. Step-by-step service explanation using short bullet points.
7. **Benefits, Outcomes, or Advantages**: Dual headings. Measurable/trustable gains.
8. **Cost / Pricing Section**: Dual headings. Straightforward starting costs and primary factors affecting the final cost.
9. **Alternatives / Comparisons**: Dual headings. Comparison with other solutions.
10. **Aftercare / Maintenance / Usage**: Dual headings. Post-service advice.
11. **Trust & E-E-A-T Signals**: Dual headings. Local expertise, certifications, awards, or expert credentials.
12. **Local Context / Service Area**: Dual headings. Suburbs, landmarks, and regional relevance.
13. **Testimonial Section**: Dual headings. Social proof.
14. **FAQ Section**: Dual headings. 5–10 LLM-optimized questions tightly focused on the target search intent.
15. **CTA Section**: Dual headings. Includes integrated booking form/contact layout.

#### Option B: Premium Direct-Response Homepage
1. **Hero**: Outcome-focused dual headings. Include exactly three high-impact reassurance bullets and clear CTAs.
2. **Trust Section**: Dual headings. Short, punchy proof of local focus, business history, credentials, guarantees, payment terms, or process confidence.
3. **Who We Are / About Section**: Dual headings. Keep this section concise and fact-led. Avoid repeating the hero.
4. **Emotional Concerns / Pain Points**: Dual headings. Speak directly to the customer's real worries using empathetic problem-led headings and short copy that names the concern, then reassures the reader.
5. **The Market Has Changed / Process**: Dual headings. A conversational overview explaining the shift in the market or why your solution is the superior approach.
